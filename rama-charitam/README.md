source Itihāsa/Purāṇa. These are `\input` by the root `ramayana-ratnakara-mulam.tex` under the
book's first part, राम-चरितानि.

`raghuvamsha/` also has two files not currently `\input` by the master document —
`raghuvamsha-transformed.tex` and `raghuvamsha-wikisource.tex` — kept as earlier/alternate
working drafts of the same content (compare against the `-orig`-style pairs seen in sibling
StotraSamhita repos).

Where a subfolder has only one file, its `\chapt{}` title is often just the source Purāṇa's own
name (used as a section divider in the compiled book) rather than a specific episode title — the
filename is usually the better guide to what the episode actually is.
