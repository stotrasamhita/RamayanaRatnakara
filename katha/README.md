# katha/ — रामायणान्तर्गताः कथाः

Side-stories and episodes embedded within the Rāmāyaṇa narrative (as opposed to `rama-charitam/`,
which is Rāma's story *as retold within* other texts) — one subfolder per source Purāṇa. `\input`
by the root `ramayana-ratnakara-mulam.tex` under the book's second part.

`skanda-puranam/` is the largest source here — ten episodes, mostly māhātmyams of the various
liṅgas/tīrthas Rāma and his companions are said to have established around Rāmeśvaram after
building the Setu.
